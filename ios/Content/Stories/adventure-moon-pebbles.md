# The Three Moon Pebbles

Captain Tumblebeard's ship lantern runs on moon pebbles, and the captain dropped all three on the way
to visit the family. The crew finds them so the lantern can glow and the ship can sail home.

## For the grown-up

- **Fits:** a backyard or a small park. Three spots a few steps to a short walk apart.
- **Placing:** put each chest on a spot you can see and know well. Choose places the child can reach
  without crossing a road, water, a car park or anything you'd have to hold their hand over.
- **Hints:** each treasure has a sentence to finish. Name a thing the child can see and recognise
  when they get there. Never write a direction ("go left", "past the gate").
  - Treasure 1: "Look near the ___." Fill in a thing: *big tree*, *flower pot*.
  - Treasure 2: "Look by something ___." Fill in a color: *red*, *yellow*.
  - Treasure 3: "Look where you ___." Fill in something the child does there: *swing high*, *play in
    the sand*.
- **Stopping point:** the finale ends with "Back to the ship!" Point to your door, car, picnic blanket
  or bench as "the ship", so the hunt ends where you want it to.

## Read-through

What the child hears, in order, with the guide's shared lines from `guide.md`.

1. **Captain:** "Ahoy, Emmanuel! It's me, Captain Tumblebeard!"
2. **Captain:** "Oh dear, oh dear. I lost something again."
3. **Captain:** "My ship's lantern needs three moon pebbles. I dropped them!"
4. **Captain:** "Can you help me find them?" "Stay with Dad. Let's go!"
5. **Captain:** "Here's a clue, matey." "Look near the big tree."
6. *(Walking. The parent leads.)* **Captain:** "Ooh! You're getting close!"
7. **Captain:** "Here it is! Tap Dig, matey!" *(Emmanuel taps **Dig!**)*
8. **Captain:** "Ooooh! Look what's inside!" "A blue moon pebble! It's yawning. Shh!"
9. **Captain:** "On to the next treasure!" "Listen close. Here's a clue." "Look by something red."
10. *(GPS drifts.)* **Captain:** "The map's a bit foggy here, matey!" *(Dad spots the chest by the red
    slide and taps **We found it**.)*
11. **Captain:** "Dad spotted it! Hooray!" "Treasure! You found it!" "A gold moon pebble! It's giggling.
    Hee hee!"
12. **Captain:** "More treasure is waiting!" "Nib whispered a clue to me." "Look where you swing high."
13. **Captain:** "Nib is dancing! Treasure is close!" "X marks the spot! Dig, dig, dig!"
14. **Captain:** "Wow! Look at that!" "The silver moon pebble! It twinkles like a star!"
15. **Finale:** "Click, click, click! All three pebbles." "Look! The lantern is glowing!" "Now my ship
    can find its way home." "Back to the ship!"
16. **Captain:** "You did it, Emmanuel!" "Into your chest they go. What a crew!"
17. **Captain:** "Now it's your turn, Emmanuel!" "Make a treasure for Dad." "Then hide it. Can Dad
    find it?"
