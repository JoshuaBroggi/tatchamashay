# Nib's Shell Party

It's Nib's birthday, but the wind blew the party things off the ship. The crew finds the drum, the
hat and the cupcake so Nib can have a party.

## For the grown-up

- **Fits:** a backyard or a small park. Three spots a few steps to a short walk apart.
- **Placing:** put each chest on a spot you can see and know well. Choose places the child can reach
  without crossing a road, water, a car park or anything you'd have to hold their hand over.
- **Hints:** each treasure has a sentence to finish. Describe the place, never the way there.
  - Treasure 1: "Look under the ___." Fill in a thing: *table*, *bench*.
  - Treasure 2: "Look next to the ___." Fill in a thing: *watering can*, *swing*.
  - Treasure 3: "Look somewhere ___." Fill in how the place feels: *shady and cool*, *full of
    flowers*.
- **Finale:** invite the child to sing and blow out the candle with you. It's a good moment to
  stop.
- **Stopping point:** "Back to the ship for the party!" Use your door, car or picnic blanket as the
  ship. A real snack there makes a lovely ending.

## Read-through

What the child hears, in order, with the guide's shared lines from `guide.md`.

1. **Captain:** "Well, shiver my shells! Emmanuel is here!"
2. **Captain:** "I need your help, matey."
3. **Captain:** "It's Nib's birthday! But the party things are lost."
4. **Captain:** "Can you help me find them?" "Stay with Mom. Let's go!"
5. **Captain:** "Here's a clue, matey." "Look under the bench."
6. **Captain:** "My beard is tingling. It's near!" "Here it is! Tap Dig, matey!"
7. **Captain:** "Ooooh! Look what's inside!" "A clam drum! Boom, boom, boom!"
8. **Captain:** "Let's find the next one!" "Listen close. Here's a clue." "Look next to the swing."
9. **Captain:** "Ooh! You're getting close!" "X marks the spot! Dig, dig, dig!"
10. **Captain:** "Treasure! You found it!" "A starfish hat! It's just Nib's size!"
11. **Captain:** "More treasure is waiting!" "Nib whispered a clue to me." "Look somewhere full of
    flowers."
12. *(Mom taps **We found it**.)* **Captain:** "Great eyes, crew! Let's open it!" "Wow! Look at that!"
    "A sea foam cupcake! With a candle!"
13. **Finale:** "Drum! Hat! Cupcake! We have everything." "Let's sing for Nib. Happy birthday, Nib!"
    "Now blow out the candle. Whoosh!" "Back to the ship for the party!"
14. **Captain:** "You did it, Emmanuel!" "Into your chest they go. What a crew!"
15. **Captain:** "Bye bye, Emmanuel! Sail safe!" "Nib waves bye bye with a claw!"
